Birthday Boy You — Mellow Update

This update changes the direction from an action-game-like party to a quieter, slightly sad birthday experience.

Added:
- Japanese / English / Simplified Chinese / Korean
- Automatic first-launch language selection from the OS locale
- Manual language selection in Settings
- Saved language and microphone sensitivity
- Completely redesigned, quieter menu
- Dedicated microphone settings
- Sensitivity slider
- Auto recalibration
- Live microphone test
- Removed technical debug numbers from normal gameplay
- CJK-capable SystemFont fallback
- Softer original ambient BGM
- Party guests remain, but their dialogue is quieter and more intimate
- After blowing out the birthday candles, cake becomes interactable
- Click and drag a cake piece toward the bottom-center of the screen to eat it
- Each piece takes two bites
- You can stop with the "I'm full / ごちそうさま" button
- Birthday ending happens after the cake scene

Install over the current project:
1. Close Godot.
2. Put BirthdayBoyYou_mellow_update.zip on Desktop.
3. Run:

   cd ~/Desktop
   cp -R BirthdayBoyYou BirthdayBoyYou_before_mellow_update
   unzip -o BirthdayBoyYou_mellow_update.zip -d BirthdayBoyYou
   open ~/Desktop/BirthdayBoyYou/project.godot

Recommended first test:
- Settings -> Microphone
- Auto adjust
- Test microphone
- Then Birthday mode
- Blow out the candles
- Drag a cake piece toward the lower center of the screen
