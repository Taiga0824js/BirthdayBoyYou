Birthday Boy You - custom audio

Put your prepared audio files in this exact folder:

assets/audio/custom/

Use these filenames:

title.mp3
    ONE BGM used continuously for both the title intro and the main menu.
    There is no separate menu track anymore, so the music will not overlap.

candle_out.wav  (or .mp3 / .ogg)
    Candle extinguish SE.

applause.wav  (or .mp3 / .ogg)
    Applause after the final birthday candle goes out.

eat.wav  (or .mp3 / .ogg)
    Cake eating SE.

menu.mp3 is no longer required and is intentionally ignored.

If a custom sound is missing, the built-in fallback is used.
After copying files here, open Godot and wait for audio import to finish.
